## Shot details

### Healthy reality

Use a 1920×1080 browser window. Hide bookmarks and unrelated tabs. Keep the command bar legible and hold for two seconds on **6 registered**.

### Shock

Move the cursor deliberately to the featured-shock control. Click once. Do not cut before the wave reaches orders and customers. Let the metric count-up settle.

### Semantic investigation

Keep the browser-agent surface and application both visible only if text remains legible. Briefly show tool names in the browser’s Site tools activity. Prioritize `trace_impact` because its violet path makes semantic actuation visually obvious.

### Branchspace

Let all three ribbons settle. Scrub once from T+0 toward T+14 and select Cost Guard, then Balanced. Avoid rapid card scrolling.

### Human intent

Click the Apex order, then **Protect this order**. Hold on the gold seal, upstream wave, amber stale cards, and capability count change. This is the key differentiator; allocate at least 10 seconds.

### Approval

Let the staged summary remain on screen long enough to read cost, protected revenue, delayed orders, and hard constraints. Click approval visibly. Cut to the Capability Dock only after `apply_plan` actually registers.

### Commit

Avoid a jump cut between tool call and changed state. Show the selected future convergence and then the verified evidence panel.

### Causal Proof

Frame proof steps 1–7 and the graph path together. The Voltra incompatibility and Helix transfer should be readable.

### End card

Use `submission/thumbnail.svg` or a clean app end frame with:

```text
BRANCHLINE
Fork reality.
Simulate the consequences.
Commit the future.
```

## Recording quality

- 1920×1080, 30 or 60 fps;
- clear cursor, no cursor trails;
- application at 100% zoom unless integrated-browser split requires 90%;
- no copyrighted music;
- optional procedural app sounds only;
- clean voiceover with captions;
- no third-party logos in the application;
- no fabricated URL or hidden approval bypass;
- final render below 2:45.
