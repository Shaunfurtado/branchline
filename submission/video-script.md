# BRANCHLINE — 2:41 demonstration script

## 0:00–0:10 — Healthy reality

**Picture:** Healthy Asterion Mobility network; six site tools registered.

**Narration:**

Operational disruptions are rarely one broken node. They are chains across suppliers, inventory, factories, products, and customer commitments.

## 0:10–0:24 — Shock

**Picture:** Human clicks **Trigger featured supplier shock**. The NoriCell shock wave propagates to cells, plants, products, orders, and customers.

**Narration:**

BRANCHLINE turns that chain into one live operational twin shared by a person and their browser agent. This synthetic outage exposes sixteen orders, two plants, and twenty-eight point four three million dollars of revenue—computed from the graph.

## 0:24–0:47 — Semantic investigation

**Picture:** Agent invokes snapshot, impact trace, constraints, and substitutes. Violet traces focus the inspected paths.

**Narration:**

Instead of reading pixels and guessing which controls to click, the agent invokes the application’s actual semantics. Each call reads the same state the person sees, and each investigation is rendered visibly in the operational world.

## 0:47–1:11 — Fork futures

**Picture:** Agent creates Service First, Cost Guard, and Balanced Recovery. Branchspace opens; scrub T+0 to T+14.

**Narration:**

The agent forks three materially different recovery futures and runs a deterministic thirty-day simulation for each. Service First maximizes delivery. Cost Guard accepts lower-priority delays. Balanced Recovery combines compatible suppliers, inventory movement, and resequencing. The recommendation is structured evidence, not a text-only answer.

## 1:11–1:36 — Human intent changes shared reality

**Picture:** Human protects Apex order `order_1082`. Gold pin and upstream wave appear. Branches fracture amber and tools unregister. Agent re-reads and re-simulates Balanced.

**Narration:**

Now the person changes intent directly. Protecting the Apex Health order increments the shared context, makes every old future stale, and revokes staging and execution capabilities. The agent’s next call encounters that exact change and replans—using scarce compatible supply for Apex and moving a lower-priority commitment instead.

## 1:36–1:58 — Stage and approve

**Picture:** Agent stages Balanced. Approval surface shows metrics, actions, hashes, compatibility, reversibility. Human clicks **Approve and unlock execution**. `apply_plan` registers.

**Narration:**

The agent can stage a plan, but it cannot approve one. Consequential execution is not merely disabled; the apply tool does not exist until a person approves the exact, current simulated diff. That approval is bound to the context and simulation hash.

## 1:58–2:22 — Commit and verify

**Picture:** Agent calls `apply_plan`; selected future converges into the live network. Agent calls `verify_plan`; recovery panel opens.

**Narration:**

After approval, the future becomes reality. BRANCHLINE creates a checkpoint, applies the linked actions transactionally, then verifies the result against its promise. In this run, ninety-three point four percent of exposed revenue is protected, cost stays below three hundred thousand dollars, Apex arrives at T plus one, and hard violations remain zero.

## 2:22–2:35 — Causal Proof

**Picture:** Agent invokes `explain_tradeoff`; numbered path highlights Nori, battery cells, ORION, Voltra rejection, Helix allocation, transfer, Apex, and counterfactual delay.

**Narration:**

Every tradeoff remains explainable. Causal Proof shows why Voltra was rejected for ORION, where compatible capacity came from, what inventory moved, and which lower-priority order changed in the counterfactual.

## 2:35–2:41 — End card

**Picture:** BRANCHLINE title and tagline.

**Narration:**

BRANCHLINE: fork reality, simulate the consequences, commit the future.
